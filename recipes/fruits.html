Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, creamy ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella. It is delicious and perfect for family dinners.

This recipe will guide you through preparing a homemade lasagna using simple ingredients and easy cooking steps.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 700 g tomato sauce
  • 250 g ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Cook the ground beef and onion in a pan until the meat is browned.
  4. Add the garlic and tomato sauce, then simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Mix the ricotta cheese, egg, Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  6. Spread some meat sauce in a baking dish. Add noodles, cheese mixture, and more sauce. Repeat the layers.
  7. Top with mozzarella cheese and bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.
